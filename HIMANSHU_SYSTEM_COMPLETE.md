## 💡 Quick Tips (Make This Work)

### For You (Managing Himanshu)

✅ **Send briefs every evening** (Mon/Tue/Wed @ 8 PM)
- Gets him a full evening to prep
- Work starts fresh next morning
- No guessing what to do

✅ **Review dashboard every Friday**
- Takes 3 minutes
- Send feedback same day
- He adjusts next week

✅ **Be specific in feedback**
- Not: "Better writing needed"
- But: "This paragraph has too many words. Cut to 1 sentence."

✅ **Praise good work**
- "Page structure was perfect" → he'll repeat it
- "90 Rank Math score, great execution"

✅ **Unblock fast**
- He messages "blocker" → you respond <2 hours
- Don't let him get stuck waiting

### For Himanshu (Content Production)

✅ **Follow the templates exactly**
- Page structure: Hero + Why + 5 Steps + FAQ + CTA + Internal links
- Blog structure: Intro + Main + Takeaway + CTA + Related
- No deviation until he masters this

✅ **Check Rank Math before publish**
- Must be ≥70
- If <70, he fixes it (you show him how once)

✅ **Internal links are non-negotiable**
- ≥3 per page, to pillar pages only
- Not to random blogs (that waters down authority)

✅ **Mark dashboard done** at EOD
- You rely on it for stats
- If it's not updated, you think work isn't done

✅ **Ask questions early**
- "What's the word count?" → ask in brief, not at EOD
- Blockers surface immediately, not after 2 hours of wrong work
