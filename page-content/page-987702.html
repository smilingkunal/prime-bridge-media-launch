What makes YouTube Ads different from every other channel

YouTube ads aren't banner ads, search ads, or social posts. They're video — and video changes everything.

  • Trust on sight. A 30-second video of your clinic, restaurant, or gym builds more trust than 10 pages of text.
  • Pre-sold leads. Someone who's watched your 60-second video is 3x more likely to book than someone who only saw your text ad.
  • Mobile-first consumption. 70% of YouTube watch time in India is on mobile.
  • Audio + visual. The combination of seeing + hearing beats reading alone.
  • Skippable = honest. You only pay for engaged viewers.
