Why most Tricity businesses fail with Meta Ads

1. They're running "engagement" campaigns, not conversion campaigns

The default is "Engagement" — the worst objective for business. It optimizes for likes, not leads.

What we do: Run Lead Generation, Messages, Store Traffic, or Conversions campaigns.

2. Their targeting is too broad

"Chandigarh, 18-65" = 1.2 million people. The algorithm can't optimize.

What we do: Tight targeting at the start, then let Meta find lookalikes after 2-3 weeks.

3. They have weak creative

Stock photos, generic copy, no clear offer. People scroll past in 0.5 seconds.

What we do: 4-6 creative variations, hook in first 3 seconds, specific offers.

4. They have a broken landing page

Click "Free consultation" ad, land on your homepage, bounce.

What we do: Dedicated landing pages, one offer, one form, one CTA.

5. They don't track conversions

No Meta Pixel, no events, flying blind.

What we do: Full Meta Pixel + CAPI setup with ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Lead, Purchase events.

FAQ

Facebook or Instagram?

Both, ideally. The audience overlap is huge. We run both from one campaign and let Meta optimize.

Do I need a big following?

No. Organic following and paid ads are separate. You can have 200 followers and run Meta Ads to 100,000 targeted people.

Can I run ads from my personal Facebook?

No. You need Meta Business Suite + Facebook Page + ad account. We set this up for you.

How long until I get leads?

Week 1 possible. Whether they're good leads depends on your offer and sales process.

What's the difference between "boosting" and running Meta Ads?

Boosting = ₹500 to show your post to your followers + their friends. Almost always a waste. Real Meta Ads = custom audience, custom creative, conversion objective, proper tracking, ongoing optimization.

Can you do WhatsApp ads specifically?

Yes. WhatsApp ads (click-to-WhatsApp) are some of our highest-converting. Average cost per conversation: ₹15-50.

What about Instagram Reels ads?

Yes, and they often outperform feed ads.
