What is Local SEO — and why Tricity businesses need it

Local SEO is the process of making your business show up when someone in your area searches for the services or products you sell. When a person in Chandigarh searches "dentist near me" or "best restaurant in Mohali" or "gym in Panchkula", Google's local 3-pack shows three businesses at the top of the search results. Local SEO gets you into that 3-pack.

The 3-pack is the most valuable real estate in search. It sits above every organic result. It shows your business name, rating, hours, and a tap-to-call button. The businesses that win it get 44% of all clicks on local search.

If you're a Tricity business and you're not in the 3-pack for your core search terms, your competitors are. And they're getting your customers.

What we do — our 6-part local SEO system

1. Google Business Profile optimization (the foundation)

Your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the #1 ranking factor for local search. We optimize every field:

  • Business name, address, phone (NAP) — verified consistent across 50+ directories
  • Primary + secondary categories chosen from Google's taxonomy
  • Service list with descriptions targeting your actual offerings
  • Business hours, attributes (women-owned, wheelchair-accessible, etc.)
  • Weekly Google Posts (offers, updates, events)
  • Photo + video upload (business interior, team, work samples)

2. Citation building + NAP consistency

We audit your existing citations, build new ones in 30+ high-authority Indian directories, and ensure NAP is 100% identical across every directory.

3. On-page local SEO

We optimize title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2 headings, schema markup, internal linking, image alt text, and page speed — all with local intent keywords.

4. Review velocity + reputation management

We set up SMS/email automations to generate 2-4 new reviews per month, respond to every review within 24 hours, and flag fake reviews.

5. Local link building

We earn links from local news (Chandigarh Tribune, Tricity News), Chamber of Commerce, local events, and partner businesses.

6. Local content + Map pack domination

City-specific service pages, "best [service] near me" content, local case studies, neighborhood-specific landing pages.
